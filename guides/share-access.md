# Share access

You can share access to your projects with another user. To do this, go to the "Dashboard" and click "Manage collaborators" next to the selected project.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>\</p></figcaption></figure>

You will be redirected to a page where you can add or remove users by user name or email.
